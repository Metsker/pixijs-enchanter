# Enchantment catalogue

54 enchants across 4 pools × 2 layers (+ Unique). Numbers are placeholders for balancing; lock the EFFECTS now, tune the values during playtesting.

Notation: `Pools = {Weapons, Armor, Jewelry, Unique}`. Layer is strict (Main XOR Utility). Unique enchants only fit the tier-7 unique slot.

## Weapons - Main (8)

All Weapons-only. All are passive damage profiles that modify the weapon's auto-attack.

| Name | Effect | Damage type |
|---|---|---|
| Sharpness | +N physical damage per swing | physical |
| Pyroclasm | +N fire damage per swing, built-in Burn chance | fire |
| Frostbite | +N cold damage per swing, built-in Freeze chance | cold |
| Voltaic | +N lightning damage per swing, built-in Shock chance | lightning |
| Envenom | +N chaos damage per swing, large built-in Poison chance | chaos |
| Smite | +N physical; +X% bonus vs enemies above 80% HP | physical |
| Bane | +N chaos; +X% bonus vs enemies below 30% HP | chaos |
| Resonance | +N damage split across ALL FIVE types per swing | mixed |
